# Kamal Selections

## Digital Storefront & Local Search Growth Platform

### PRD v1.0

---

## 1. Product Vision

Build a premium, mobile-first digital storefront for **Kamal Selections** that transforms an almost entirely offline clothing business into a discoverable local fashion brand.

The website's primary purpose is not e-commerce.

It is:

**Discover → Trust → Explore → Contact → Visit Store**

The long-term objective is to establish Kamal Selections as a recognizable women's and kids' fashion business in Shadnagar while creating a digital foundation that can support future growth.

### Brand positioning

**Kamal Selections**
**Fashion for Every Woman & Every Little One**

Founded in **2021**.

Physical store in **Ibrahim Complex, Main Road, Shadnagar, Telangana**.

Primary categories:

* Women's Wear
* Kids Wear

Core commercial advantage:

**Affordable/value-focused fashion.**

# 8. V1 Product Philosophy

## The website is NOT an inventory system.

The physical store contains 1,000+ products and there is currently no practical stock-management workflow.

Therefore V1 will use:

**Fashion showcase → enquiry → store visit**

rather than:

**Product → inventory → cart → checkout**

### V1 will NOT require:

* SKU management
* Stock counts
* Inventory database
* Cart
* Online payment
* Customer accounts
* Delivery management
* Order tracking
* Stock synchronization

# 10. Product Enquiry System

There will be no traditional checkout.

Primary CTAs:

### Call Store

Direct phone call.

### WhatsApp

Open WhatsApp conversation.

### Visit Store

Open Google Maps directions.

### Enquire

A general enquiry route can eventually combine WhatsApp/contact.

For individual visual showcases, wording should avoid guaranteeing stock.

Preferred examples:

* **Ask About This Style**
* **Enquire on WhatsApp**
* **Visit Store**
* **Call Store**

Avoid universal wording such as:

**"Buy Now"**

or

**"Check Stock"**

because V1 does not guarantee live inventory.

# 28. V1 Non-Functional Requirements

The website should be:

### Mobile-first

Primary priority.

### Fast

Avoid unnecessary heavy animation libraries/assets.

### SEO-friendly

Content must remain crawlable.

### Accessible

Readable typography, sufficient contrast, keyboard support where relevant.

### Secure

No unnecessary backend attack surface.

### Maintainable

You should be able to modify content without rebuilding the entire application.

### Scalable

Architecture should permit future:

* Product catalog
* CMS
* WhatsApp ordering
* Inventory
* Checkout
* Customer accounts
* Analytics
* Offers
