# 1. Core Search Strategy

The website should establish one clear entity:

**Kamal Selections**

with three primary associations:

**Shadnagar**

**Women's Wear**

**Kids Wear**

The site should then build supporting topical relationships around the actual categories offered.

```text
                         KAMAL SELECTIONS
                                │
                 ┌──────────────┴──────────────┐
                 │                             │
             SHADNAGAR                  FASHION STORE
                 │                             │
        ┌────────┴────────┐             ┌──────┴──────┐
        │                 │             │             │
   WOMEN'S WEAR       KIDS WEAR     WOMEN'S       KIDS'
                                      CATEGORIES    CATEGORIES
```

This is the foundation for local search, answer engines, and generative search visibility.

# 31. "Best" Keyword Strategy

Users may search:

* best dress shop in Shadnagar
* best ladies wear in Shadnagar
* best kids wear in Shadnagar

We can optimize relevant pages for these search intents.

But the website should not make an unsupported factual claim such as:

**"We are the #1 best dress shop in Shadnagar."**

Instead, content should demonstrate why a customer might consider the store:

* Categories
* Location
* Affordability
* Store information
* Actual products
* Genuine reviews once available

---

# 32. Competitor Strategy

The competitor's digital presence should be treated as a benchmark for:

* Content frequency
* Video quality
* Photography
* Google visibility
* Social engagement
* Website usability

Not as something to copy.

Kamal Selections needs its own recognizable visual identity.

# 34. Typography

Recommended structure:

### Display

Elegant serif or high-fashion display type.

### Body

Clean modern sans-serif.

### UI

Highly readable sans-serif.

The logo itself should never be recreated as text when the actual logo asset is available.

---

# 35. Image Strategy

There are two separate visual systems.

## Editorial imagery

Can be AI-generated.

Used for:

* Hero
* Category storytelling
* Fashion campaigns
* Instagram content

## Business evidence

Must preferably be real.

Used for:

* Store
* Signboard
* Interior
* Actual products
* Google Business Profile

This distinction is critical for trust.

# 36. Homepage Animation Timeline

The homepage should feel cinematic but not like a movie website.

### Load

### Scroll 2

### Scroll 4

# 41. What We Should NOT Do

Do not:

* Create 500 AI SEO pages
* Stuff "best dress shop Shadnagar" everywhere
* Fake reviews
* Fake ratings
* Claim #1 without evidence
* Claim stock availability
* Use AI store photos as actual store photos
* Create fake customer testimonials
* Copy competitor content
* Build an unnecessary backend
* Build checkout before demand exists
* Sacrifice page speed for animations
